4-Stage Pipeline

How Aneevarp DocAI Works

A high-performance pipeline turning dense unstructured documents into verified, cited intelligence.

01

Multi-Format Ingest

Extracts raw text, structured tables, and embedded hidden URLs from PDFs, Word (`.docx`), and `.txt` files with zero data loss.

PyMuPDF & docx
02

Vectorization & FAISS

Chunks text semantically with overlap and converts chunks into numerical vectors using Google Gemini Embeddings in memory.

In-Memory FAISS
03

Gemini 2.5 Reasoning

Retrieves top semantic context and feeds it into Gemini 2.5 Flash for high-speed multi-turn reasoning and JSON output.

Gemini 2.5 Flash
04

Visual Grounding

Calculates X/Y coordinate bounding boxes for the exact quote and renders a cropped visual snippet proving the answer.

Zero Hallucination
